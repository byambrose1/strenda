Good questions

I've never done strength training. Is this for me?

Yes. Strenda Club is for beginners and for people coming back after a break. Every exercise is filmed and explained, and your plan starts where you are.

Do I need to be fit already?

No. You don't have to arrive disciplined, confident or fit. That is what the group and your coach are for.

What equipment do I need?

A sturdy chair and a little space. Hand weights or full water bottles help later but are optional.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. Your camera is optional. You can follow along and ask questions without being on screen. If you turn it on, your coach can check your technique.

When are the sessions?

Group sessions are planned for Monday 7.30pm, Tuesday 9.30am or Wednesday 7.30pm (you choose one), plus the club session on Saturday at 9am, which is recorded. Times are confirmed by 5 January.

Is the £10 really refundable?

Yes. Ask any time before your group starts on 12 January and it goes back to your card within 5 working days. It's also refunded automatically if the times don't suit you, if the health check shows it isn't right for you yet, or if we don't start on 12 January.

What happens if I miss a session?

Your coach will message you within a working day to help you back in, with an easier option if you need one. You won't be told off. Life happens.

I'm losing weight. Is this a diet programme?

No. Strenda is about building strength, whatever your goal. If you're losing weight in any way, we'll help you build strength alongside it. We don't give advice on medication or diets; your GP, pharmacist or dietitian is the right person for that.

I have a health condition or an injury. Can I join?

Often, yes. We'll ask about your health before you start, and we may ask you to check with your GP first. Exercises are adapted where appropriate.

Is it only for women?

Our first groups are mostly women aged 40 to 60, because that's who has asked for it. Everyone is welcome to join the list, and we plan to open more groups.

How do I cancel?

Any time, in a couple of clicks. You keep your place until the end of the month you've paid for.
